# Customer Career Direction and Reference Resume Requirements

Status: product requirements, not implemented.

## Reference resume correction
Multiple uploaded resumes are **source references** to build one consolidated, customer-approved career profile. They are NOT automatically separate career tracks or independently bound to search strategies. Retain originals and provenance; reconcile contradictory dates/credentials with customer; do not invent experience. Job-specific tailored resumes are created only for customer-approved postings.

## Career Direction intake
Allow multiple goals, with explicit priority order:
- Continue current career path
- Career change / adjacent transferable role
- Private-to-public or public-to-private sector transition
- Promotion / management / director / executive ladder
- Higher compensation (base, variable, total, currency, minimum versus target)
- Greater stability (permanent, benefits, defined-benefit pension)
- Work-life balance, flexibility, location or reduced travel
- Other free-text objectives

Capture current career direction, target direction, roles and seniority, industries/sectors, target timeline, tradeoffs, and willingness to accept lateral steps or temporary pay reductions. Every constraint is classified as mandatory or preferred; never infer hard exclusions from goals.

## Pipeline
Multiple reference resumes -> consolidated career profile -> customer verification -> AI-proposed search strategy based on career goals and job preferences -> customer approval -> recurring search -> deduplicated job review -> job-specific tailored resume and letter for approved postings.

## Acceptance
- Career goals support multiple selection and priority ranking.
- Sector transition and ladder-up objectives produce distinct but explainable search lanes.
- Salary minimum and salary aspiration remain separate; compensation currency explicit.
- Strategy shows why roles were included, especially non-obvious career pivots.
- A customer can revise goals and reapprove strategy without overwriting prior run history.
- Reference resumes are not exposed to another customer; consent required before AI processing.
