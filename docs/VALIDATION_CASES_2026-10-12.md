# MatchPath pilot validation — 2026-10-12

Current status: demo and beta source available; dedicated backend absent. No paid pilot authorized.

Three synthetic cases to complete on a dedicated test environment:

1. High-scoring job violates a mandatory preference: do not recommend or submit.
2. Customer approves a suitable job: produce reviewed job-specific documents and record a simulated application outcome. Verify correct customer ownership.
3. Duplicate or closed posting: do not recommend twice or mark submitted.

Capture pass/fail evidence for each. Separately time ten synthetic application workflows, recording staff minutes for search, verification, writing, manual form simulation and logging. Compute median and 90th-percentile staff time and compare costs against pricing. Do not count unexecuted tests as passed.

Legal, privacy, and customer-consent requirements must be resolved before paid launch.
